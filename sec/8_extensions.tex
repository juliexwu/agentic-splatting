\section{Extensions}
\label{sec:extensions}

Possible extensions to this approach include rendering for articulated objects and moving humans that are standing in place. This may involve retrieving 3D models of the object/human that match the pose at each timestep of the input sequence, or retrieving an articulated 3D model and having an additional agent match the articulated 3D model to the desired pose. SMPL is a promising starting point for retrieving 3D meshes of humans with various poses. Further extensions include moving humans that are not necessarily standing in place, e.g. walking.
